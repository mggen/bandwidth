📜 Privacy Policy

"Bandwidth Saver" App

1️⃣ Introduction

Welcome to "Bandwidth Saver" app. Your privacy is our top priority. This app is designed to help you manage your monthly data consumption efficiently, while ensuring complete privacy and data security.

2️⃣ Data Collection

🔒 We do not collect any personal data at all.
  • ❌ We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information
  • ❌ We do not collect your IP address
  • ❌ We do not track your geographic location
  • ❌ We do not use any analytics tools to track your behavior
  • ✅ Everything that happens in the app stays on your device only

3️⃣ Required Permissions

The app requires the following permissions to run its core services:

Permission Purpose
VPN Service To create a local tunnel on your device to apply speed limits to selected apps
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS To identify the currently active app for selective throttling
INTERNET Only to display ads (from Unity Ads), not to send any data about you

Note: All these permissions are used only for the stated purpose and do not give us any access to your personal data.

4️⃣ Ads (The Only Third Party)

We display ads within the app through Unity Ads network, which is the only third party that interacts with the app.

For more information, you can review Unity Technologies' Privacy Policy:

🔗 Unity Privacy Policy

5️⃣ Data Security

Since the app runs locally on your device and does not transmit your data over the internet, the risk of data breaches is extremely limited.

6️⃣ Children's Privacy

Our app does not target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from this age group.
